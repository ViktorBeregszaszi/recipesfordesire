The workshop

Fantasies and desires can be hard to share, even with the person you love

Many people in a relationship carry fantasies they have never dared to share with their partner. Keeping quiet can feel safer than risking an awkward silence, even years in.

Recipes for Desire is a card game we designed to make these conversations easy, playful and deeply connecting. In our workshop, couples find the desires they share, bring back the spark and grow together towards what brings them aliveness.

It fits the slot in your programme for couples who want to deepen their connection.

What couples experience

Four steps towards a sexy date idea, through a metaphor

Creating a fantasy together is a little like cooking a meal for someone you love. You find out what they crave, leave out what they cannot eat, and put your heart into the dish.

  1. Arrive

    We start by setting intentions and taking deep breaths together. Then a round of warm-up games helps participants notice their own yes and no, before anything more personal comes up.

  2. Remove the sensitivities

    Each couple gets a deck of fantasy cards, each card one ingredient for a sexy date: a place, an activity, a tool. Whatever is not for you gets discarded.

  3. Find your favourites, season with maybes

    Together you discover the cards you all said hell yes to. And the maybes become gentle conversations about desires, curiosities and what could make them a yes.

  4. Cook your recipe

    You combine your favourite ingredients into a date idea with a starter, a main course and a dessert. You describe it to each other in vivid detail, then write it into your Recipe Book to take home.

Why it works

Think less, feel more, speak from the heart

Out of the head, into the senses

In tantra, presence comes first. Talking about desire can pull people into their heads. With the questions and steps printed on the cards, couples can slow down, listen and feel.

Why we cook

Desire is a tender subject, and a metaphor gives it a little distance. “That ingredient is not for me” is easier to say, and to hear, than turning down a partner’s fantasy. And everyone already knows how to talk about food.

Get familiar with your yeses and nos

In the heat of the moment, the wish to please can blur a no. The game gives couples time to feel into a fantasy before it happens. That protects their boundaries, and it can show them a yes they never let themselves explore.

Who we are

A game designer and a storyteller, who made this game for themselves first

It started with a book. We were reading about intimate fantasies, and at the back was a list of desires to mark yes, no or maybe.

But the list was boringly static, so Viktor grabbed a pad of post-it notes and turned it into cards. Suddenly we could move the ideas around, combine them and play. That evening became the first game of Recipes for Desire, and it kept us talking for a whole night. It was vulnerable and beautiful, and it brought us closer.

We want couples to keep their relationship exciting, years in. The game is also practice for something bigger. Recognising your feelings and boundaries, saying what you want truthfully, and listening to others with curiosity and compassion are skills that will make this world a better place to live in for all.

For organisers

Safe for your participants, simple for you

We connect through words

Recipes for Desire is a conversation workshop. There is no nudity, intimate touch or sexual activity.

Two facilitators are present the whole time, and Rebecca is there for anyone who needs support. We work alongside your festival’s own support team. The framework draws on Nonviolent Communication, Authentic Relating and boundary work.

What we need if we run the workshop at your event

The session
  • 90 to 120 minutes, depending on your programme

The room

What we bring
  • Everything the session needs
  • A card deck for every couple
  • Recipe Books for couples to take home
