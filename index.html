The workshop in a nutshell

Most people carry a private list of intimate fantasies they would love to explore, yet never quite find the moment to say them out loud. This workshop creates that moment.

Participants arrive with a partner, or with a flirt they are exploring the festival with. They leave with:

  • Two to four adventures

    Date ideas they had never thought of, and are both excited to try. Written into a small booklet to take home.

  • Clearer language

    Words for expressing desires, boundaries and emotions, practised in the room and usable afterwards.

  • A repeatable process

    A way of navigating Yes, Maybe and No together that keeps working long after the festival.

How the workshop unfolds

Cooking, as a way of talking about desire

Using playful cooking metaphors, participants imagine, negotiate and co-create intimate date ideas. These are not regular pasta-and-ketchup dates. They come with a starter, a main course and a dessert.

Care in the room

What happens is talking

Authentic, imaginative, juicy talking, so you can explore freely and joyfully afterwards.

There is no nudity, intimate touch or sexual activity during the workshop. Anyone may pause, decline, renegotiate or step away at any time.

The game framework supporting the discussions draws on boundary work, Nonviolent Communication and Authentic Relating, so the conversations stay grounded and consensual.

Who holds the room

Viktor Beregszászi

Facilitator, game designer

Viktor combines a decade of experience in personal development and tantra with professional facilitation and learning-game design, and has taught more than 1,500 people over the years. He holds playful, brave spaces where people tap into their authentic creativity.

Rebecca Langley Jensen

Co-facilitator, actor

Rebecca is an actor working with storytelling and embodiment. She has practised tantra for two years, using the learnings in her work and private life. She supports participants in crafting vivid, personal fantasies, and co-facilitates with a focus on emotional support.
